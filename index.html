How it works

From application to graduation, every step is designed around a working schedule.

Apply

Submit transcripts and a short statement — no entrance exam required.

Enroll

Meet with an advisor to build a course plan around your schedule.

Study

Attend live seminars or watch recordings, on any device, at any hour.

Graduate

Walk at commencement or receive your diploma by mail — your choice.
